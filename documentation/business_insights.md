
# GeoPulse – Business Insights

## Objective
The objective is to convert GPS movement and store
proximity analysis into meaningful business insights.

## Store Performance Analysis
- Compare GPS ping activity across stores.
- Calculate catchment coverage percentage.
- Identify stores with higher nearby GPS activity.
- Compare average distance from stores.

## Time-Based Activity Analysis
- Analyse GPS pings by hour.
- Compare activity across different dayparts.
- Identify periods with higher recorded GPS activity.

## Business Applications
- Support store location planning.
- Understand location-based movement patterns.
- Help compare store catchment areas.
- Support business decisions using location data.

## Important Limitation
GPS pings indicate device location activity.
They do not confirm actual customer visits,
purchases or transactions.

## Output Files
- store_performance_analysis.csv
- time_based_activity_summary.csv
