
# GeoPulse Dashboard KPI Definitions

## Project
GeoPulse - GPS Movement and Store Activity Analytics

## Objective
To monitor GPS movement patterns, store proximity,
location activity and time-based movement through
a visual analytics dashboard.

## KPI 1 - Total GPS Pings
Definition:
Total number of GPS location records in the dataset.

Purpose:
Understand the overall volume of recorded GPS activity.

## KPI 2 - Unique Devices
Definition:
Count of distinct device IDs recorded in the dataset.

Purpose:
Understand how many unique devices are represented.

## KPI 3 - Average GPS Accuracy
Definition:
Average GPS accuracy in metres.

Purpose:
Evaluate the quality of recorded GPS location data.

## KPI 4 - Catchment Pings
Definition:
GPS pings that fall within the defined store catchment radius.

Purpose:
Measure GPS activity within store catchment areas.

## KPI 5 - Catchment Coverage Percentage
Definition:
Percentage of GPS pings falling within the store catchment radius.

Formula:
(Catchment Pings / Total Valid Pings) * 100

Purpose:
Compare GPS activity within store catchment areas.

## KPI 6 - Average Distance from Store
Definition:
Average distance between GPS pings and their nearest store.

Purpose:
Understand how close recorded GPS locations are to stores.

## KPI 7 - Peak Activity Hour
Definition:
Hour with the highest number of recorded GPS pings.

Purpose:
Identify the time period with the highest location activity.

## KPI 8 - Store Activity
Definition:
Total GPS pings assigned to each nearest store.

Purpose:
Compare recorded GPS activity around different stores.

## Important Limitation
GPS pings represent recorded device locations.
They do not confirm actual store visits, purchases,
customer identities or sales.
